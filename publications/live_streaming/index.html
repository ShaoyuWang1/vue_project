Live-Streaming Commerce: An Approach to Implementing Targeted Advertising in Sponsored Search

Key words: Research paper, mechanism design, mechanism design, targeted advertising

Shaoyu Wang
CUHK(SZ)
Pin Gao
CUHK(SZ)
Yang Li
IVEY Business School
Ying-ju Chen
HKUST Business School

The dominant technology in online advertising has been sponsored search. Publishers benefit from premium ad positions and capitalize on the vast amount of customer traffic they deliver to advertisers. However, the attention-grabbing nature of sponsored search poses a challenge to the effectiveness of advertising, as targeting is not easily aligned with the success formula of sponsored search since targeting at any ad position tends to diminish the conversion rate, thereby jeopardizing publishers’ revenue. In this paper, we argue that the contradiction between targeted advertising and sponsored search stems from the neglect of information control in ad content. If publishers can manage the information an advertiser presents to potential consumers, targeted advertising can act as a catalyst for sponsored search, resulting in greater revenues for publishers. This perspective sheds light on the thriving live-streaming advertising industry. By sequentially presenting ad content for different products through the talents of publishers or influencers, publishers can integrate targeted advertising into the sponsored search framework. That has two benefits: first, the control of ad content empowers the publisher to consistently direct a larger portion of customers to advertisers with high-profit margins regardless of their assigned ad positions. And second, targeted advertising helps advertisers to prosper from the lifetime value of matched customers. Moreover, targeted advertising is also a device of coping with information asymmetry when being applied to an inferior ad position.
